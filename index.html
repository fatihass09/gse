Our company background

Practical skill, proven on site.

Great Skills Enterprise (GSE) is a Bumiputera company established in 2014, operating from Tanjong Malim, Perak Darul Ridzuan.

We are registered with the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) and the Ministry of Finance Malaysia (MoF). Our main work is construction, especially civil engineering, together with services and supply.

GSE also runs courses and training focused on technical fields. As a certified HRDF trainer, we deliver technical and management courses, including the contractor management course Sijil Kecekapan Pengurusan (SKP).

Mission

  • Provide the best quality results
  • Apply sustainability in construction engineering in every job
  • Ensure that practice and theory are always in sync with the times

Vision

To become a company that moves along with the advancement of construction technology and training in the field of sustainability-oriented engineering.
